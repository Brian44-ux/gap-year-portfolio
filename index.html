My Projects

HealthBridge Uganda

HealthBridge Uganda is an early-stage research project exploring challenges people face when accessing healthcare. I am currently focused on understanding the problem before deciding what solution to build.

What I've Done So Far

  • Conducted 14 interviews to learn about people's experiences accessing healthcare.
  • Spoken with people from different backgrounds and listened to their experiences.
  • Researched challenges including affordability, transportation, healthcare worker access, medicine availability, and trustworthy health information.
  • Compared different experiences to look for patterns and recurring problems.
  • Learned to validate a problem before rushing to build a solution.
  • Started exploring how technology could potentially help address healthcare challenges.

What I'm Learning

One of the biggest lessons I am learning is that identifying a problem is not enough. I need to understand how painful the problem is, how people currently deal with it, and whether they would actually want or pay for a better solution.

My Learning Journey

My gap year has given me the opportunity to explore different interests and learn through experience. In February 2026, I was awarded a scholarship to participate in the YINNOH program, where I was introduced to social entrepreneurship and learned the importance of understanding a problem before trying to build a solution. This experience made me more interested in community problems and led me to research healthcare challenges through HealthBridge Uganda.

As I continued working on HealthBridge, I became interested in technology and how I could eventually build some of the ideas I was exploring. I started learning coding with HTML and CSS and began building this portfolio website from scratch.

I am still at the beginning of my learning journey, but I am enjoying the process of trying new things, making mistakes, and improving along the way. I want to continue developing my skills in technology, entrepreneurship, and problem-solving during my gap year.

My Current Hustle

Truck Turnman

During my gap year, I have been working as a truck turnman to earn a living while pursuing my other goals. The work has taught me responsibility, discipline, and the importance of being dependable.

Working has also given me the opportunity to move between different places and interact with people from different backgrounds. These experiences have helped me become more observant of the different ways people live and the challenges they face.

I am balancing this work with learning to code, researching HealthBridge Uganda, exploring social entrepreneurship, and preparing for university. My gap year is not only about learning new skills, but also about gaining real-world experience and becoming more independent.
