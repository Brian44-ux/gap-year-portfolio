My Projects

HealthBridge Uganda

HealthBridge Uganda is an early-stage research project exploring challenges people face when accessing healthcare. I am currently focused on understanding the problem before deciding what solution to build.

What I've Done So Far

  • Conducted 14 interviews to learn about people's experiences accessing healthcare.
  • Spoken with people from different backgrounds and listened to their experiences.
  • Researched challenges including affordability, transportation, healthcare worker access, medicine availability, and trustworthy health information.
  • Compared different experiences to look for patterns and recurring problems.
  • Learned to validate a problem before rushing to build a solution.
  • Started exploring how technology could potentially help address healthcare challenges.

What I'm Learning

One of the biggest lessons I am learning is that identifying a problem is not enough. I need to understand how painful the problem is, how people currently deal with it, and whether they would actually want or pay for a better solution.
